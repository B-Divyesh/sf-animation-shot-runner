Privacy

The short version

The Shot Runner CLI has no network code, tracking, or telemetry. It reads the paths you name and writes caches, frames, contact sheets, and receipts locally.

Documentation site

The manifest demo runs entirely in your browser and does not upload its contents. A restored Producer Toolkit token and its last verification result are stored in your browser’s local storage under keys beginning sb_license:animation-shot-runner. You can remove them with the “Remove license” control or your browser settings. The offline worker caches only public, same-origin documentation assets; it never stores license-bearing URLs, tokens, or entitlement verification traffic in Cache Storage.

Purchases

If you choose to buy, the checkout and license verification requests are handled by Sociobot, with Dodo as merchant of record. Their service receives the information required to complete and support the transaction. Shot Runner never receives your payment card details.

Contact

Privacy questions may be opened in the project’s public issue tracker. Do not include license tokens or sensitive media in an issue.
